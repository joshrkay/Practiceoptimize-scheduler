# Incident Response and Breach Notification Policy

> **DRAFT. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by counsel.** Notification deadlines and content must be confirmed by counsel and the executed BAAs; not yet adopted.

1. **Report.** Anyone who suspects a security incident or improper use or disclosure of PHI reports it to the security officer immediately.
2. **Triage.** The security officer records the incident, contains it (revoke credentials, isolate systems), and preserves evidence including audit logs.
3. **Assess.** Determine whether PHI was involved and perform the HIPAA four-factor risk assessment to decide whether it is a reportable breach of unsecured PHI. Decisions are documented.
4. **Notify.** Notify affected practices (covered entities) without unreasonable delay and within the period in each BAA. Covered entities decide on notice to individuals and regulators unless a BAA delegates it. Counsel is engaged before external notices.
5. **Remediate and learn.** Fix root cause, update the risk analysis, and hold a post-incident review within 30 days.
6. **Records.** Incident files are retained at least 6 years.

**Owner:** HIPAA security officer. **Review:** annually and after every incident; test with a tabletop exercise at least yearly.
