# Sanctions Policy

> **DRAFT. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by counsel.** Employment-law implications must be reviewed by counsel; not yet adopted.

1. Workforce members who violate HIPAA or company privacy and security policies are subject to sanctions proportionate to the violation.
2. Indicative tiers: inadvertent violation (retraining, documented warning); negligent or repeated violation (formal warning, access reduction); intentional or malicious violation (termination of employment or contract, and referral to authorities where required).
3. The security officer investigates, decides, and documents each sanction. No retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.
4. Records are kept 6 years.

**Owner:** HIPAA security officer. **Review:** annually.
