# Workforce Training Policy

> **DRAFT. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by counsel.** Not yet adopted.

1. Every workforce member and contractor with possible PHI access completes HIPAA privacy and security training before access is granted.
2. Refresher training is completed at least annually and after material policy or system changes.
3. Training covers: what PHI is, minimum necessary, access and device rules, phishing, incident reporting, no PHI in tickets, chat or email, and sanctions.
4. Completion is recorded (person, date, content version) and kept 6 years.
5. Access is suspended for anyone whose training has lapsed.

**Owner:** HIPAA security officer. **Review:** annually.
