This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is between [PRACTICE LEGAL NAME] ("Covered Entity") and [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("Business Associate"), effective [DATE].

## 1. Definitions
Terms such as Protected Health Information (PHI), Breach, Security Incident, Subcontractor and Minimum Necessary have the meanings in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures
Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only to provide scheduling and coordination services to Covered Entity under the services agreement, as required by law, and as this Agreement permits. Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in a way that would violate the Privacy Rule if done by Covered Entity.

## 3. Safeguards
Business Associate shall use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards and comply with the Security Rule with respect to electronic PHI.

## 4. Reporting
Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not permitted by this Agreement, any Security Incident, and any Breach of unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and no later than [NUMBER] days after discovery, with the information Covered Entity needs for notification.

## 5. Subcontractors
Business Associate shall ensure each Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI agrees in writing to the same restrictions. Current subcontractors are listed in Business Associate's subprocessor register, which Business Associate shall make available on request.

## 6. Individual rights
Business Associate shall make PHI available for access, amendment and accounting of disclosures as required by 45 CFR 164.524, 164.526 and 164.528, within [NUMBER] days of request.

## 7. Books and records
Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of HHS for compliance determinations.

## 8. Return or destruction and retention
On termination, Business Associate shall return or destroy PHI where feasible. Case PHI is deleted 90 days after case close in the ordinary course. Audit logs, which are scrubbed of PHI, are retained 6 years. If return or destruction is infeasible, protections continue.

## 9. Term and termination
This Agreement lasts as long as Business Associate holds PHI. Covered Entity may terminate for a material breach not cured within [NUMBER] days.
